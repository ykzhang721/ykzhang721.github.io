About

I am Yikai Zhang, a fourth-year Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science at Fudan University, advised by Prof. Yanghua Xiao. My work focuses on language agents and agentic models, especially reasoning and planning, post-training, and benchmark design for LLM-based agents. I am based in Shanghai and open to collaborations on autonomous agents and LLMs. Feel free to reach me by email.
